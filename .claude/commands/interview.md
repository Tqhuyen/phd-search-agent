# /interview — Prep Pack + Mock Interview

Usage: `/interview <lab>` (must match a tracked application).

## Steps
1. Load the archive `documents/applications/<lab>_<role>/`: the posting, the CV and
   SOP the interviewer will have read, and any recorded feedback.
2. Research the PI and group per `07-web-research.md` with a **verify-before-use**
   rule — every talking point traces to a source.
3. Build the prep pack per `06-interview-prep.md`:
   - Likely questions mapped to the lab's area.
   - 5–6 STAR examples drawn from the profile, quantified.
   - Honest bridge answers for known gaps (never invented experience).
   - Questions for the PI.
4. Offer a mock interview: roleplay the PI, ask from the mapped list, then give
   feedback on structure and specificity (not on research correctness).
5. Save the pack to `documents/applications/<lab>_<role>/interview.md`.
