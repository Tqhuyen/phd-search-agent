# 05 — Supervisor Outreach Email

Draft only. Never send, never schedule, never create accounts.

## Anatomy (4–6 sentences, under ~150 words)

1. **Subject:** `<specific topic> — prospective PhD applicant` (no ALL CAPS, no emoji).
2. **Who you are (1 sentence):** current degree/institution and field.
3. **The hook (1–2 sentences):** one specific reference to a recent paper or
   project of theirs, and the concrete question or idea it sparked. This is the
   part that gets a reply. Cite the paper by name.
4. **Your contribution (1 sentence):** one concrete skill or result you could
   bring to that project.
5. **The ask (1 sentence):** whether they will be taking students for the
   `<term>` intake, and whether they would consider your application.
6. **Close:** thanks, CV attached, full name and one link.

## Rules
- Reference work from the **last 12 months** where possible. Verify it exists
  (`07-web-research.md`). Never cite a paper you have not confirmed.
- One lab per email. Never bulk-send or BCC.
- No generic flattery. "Your work is impressive" is deleted. Say *what* in the
  work matters to you.
- No em-dashes, no cliches (`02-writing-style.md`).
- Attach the tailored CV; mention it. Do not attach the SOP unless asked.
- If the position is a formal ad with a portal, the email is a courtesy to the PI,
  not a substitute for the application.

## Template

```
Subject: <method/topic from their recent paper> — prospective PhD applicant

Dear Professor <Name>,

I am <name>, currently finishing <degree> at <institution>, working on <field>.
Your recent work on <specific paper/project> raised a question I keep returning
to: <one concrete research question>. I have <one concrete thing you did> that I
believe could <specific contribution to that question>.

I am preparing an application for the <term> intake and wanted to ask whether you
expect to take on students. My CV is attached.

Thank you for your time.
<name> — <one link>
```

## Follow-up
- If no reply ~10 days after confirmed sending, draft a short nudge (2 sentences).
- Drafts never change contact/follow-up dates or confirmed status. Log
  `contacted_on` / `followed_up_on` only after the user confirms sending and its
  actual date; leave dates unknown until confirmed.
