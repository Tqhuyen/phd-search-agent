# 06 — PhD Interview Preparation

Build a prep pack from the application archive (posting, CV, SOP, email, feedback).

## Likely interview shape (EU/UK/CA/AU research PhD)
1. Short research presentation (5–15 min) of past work.
2. Method deep-dive: why that method, what failed, what you would change.
3. Proposed direction for the PhD.
4. Fit / supervision: how you work, how you handle being stuck.
5. Funding / logistics / start date.
6. Your questions.

## Build STAR examples
Prepare 5–6 from real experience (frozen to the profile):
- A research result you are proud of.
- A failure or dead end and how you responded.
- A conflict or disagreement in a team.
- A time you learned a method quickly.
- A time you took ownership without being asked.

Each: **S**ituation → **T**ask → **A**ction → **R**esult (quantified).

## Research-question mapping
For the target lab, list 5 questions they are likely to ask about *their* area and
draft a 3-sentence answer each. Ground every answer in a paper you have read.

## Honest gaps
For each known gap (a method you do not know, a language level, no wet-lab
experience), prepare a **bridge answer**: acknowledge it, show the closest thing
you have done, and state a concrete plan to close it. Never invent experience.

## Questions to ask the PI
- How do you mentor students — weekly meetings, co-working, independence?
- What is the funding and its duration?
- What has the group published in the last year, and where is it heading?
- What do your recent graduates do now?
- What would you expect from me in the first six months?

## Mock interview
Offer a roleplay: the agent plays the PI, asks from the mapped list, and gives
feedback on structure and specificity (not on research correctness).
